Exploring Polymorphic Gradual Types with Phil and Peter

Phil, Peter, and I have been exploring polymoprhic gradual typing
since the COVID-19 pandemic. The improvements to Zoom removed the
geographic barrier of our homes being in Edinburgh, Rio de Janeiro,
Freiburg, and Bloomington. Our goal has been to develop a beautiful
calculus that supports interoperability between dynamically typed
regions of code and polymorphic, statically typed regions of code.
We've explored many variations on the calculus, most of them based on
a polymorphic variant of Fritz's Coercion Calculus. The latest version
has a beautiful reduction semantics, but mechanizing the gradual
guarantee is quite difficult. Even adding Codex and Claude to our
collaboration has not yet yielded the proof! Nevertheless, we've
learned a lot in the process, which I an happy to share in this talk.
