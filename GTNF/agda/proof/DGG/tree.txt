# GTNF DGG proof tree (input to dashboard.py).
#
# One item per line.  Indentation (two spaces per level) gives the
# hierarchy: an item's children are the lemmas its proof is built from.
# `uses:` lists further dependencies that live elsewhere in the tree
# (shared lemmas).  The item name N refers to the files
#   proof/DGG/NDef.agda     the statement
#   proof/DGG/NProof.agda   the proof, parameterized at module level
#   proof/DGG/N.agda        the Lemma module (the instantiation)
# and an item named Dir/N refers to proof/Dir/N{Def,Proof,}.agda.
# A `|` and text after the name is the one-line description.

DynamicGradualGuarantee | the theorem (statement at top level, §2)  uses: CatchupRight CatchupLeft CatchupBlame ImprecisionTyping TypeSafety/Progress TypeSafety/Preservation TypeSafety/Irreducible
  MultiSim | forward simulation over runs  uses: ImprecisionTyping TypeSafety/Preservation
    Sim | the more precise side steps, the less precise catches up
      SimFrame | ξ cases, including boundary frames  uses: AllocImp EvolveImp
      SimBeta | Beta, Wrap  uses: SubstImp
      SimTyBeta | TyBeta through InstX  uses: InstXImp AllocImp
      SimBoundary | Merge, Id, IdDyn  uses: MergeImp
      SimCast | CastId, CastSeq, CastSeq?, CastFun, Inst, TagUntag, TagUntagBad, TagUntagBad-⟪⟫, BlameBotIntro, Blame
      CatchupRight | a left value: the right reaches a related value
  MultiSimBack | backward simulation over runs  uses: ImprecisionTyping TypeSafety/Preservation TypeSafety/Determinism
    SimBack | the less precise side steps, both catch up
      SimBackFrame | ξ cases  uses: AllocImp EvolveImp SimBackInstX
      SimBackInstX | inside ∀⊑⟪+⟫'s premise: the right steps, the left (a ∀-value) never moves
      SimBackBeta | Beta, Wrap  uses: SubstImp
      SimBackTyBeta | TyBeta, Inst  uses: InstXImp AllocImp
      SimBackBoundary | Merge, Id, IdDyn  uses: MergeImp
      SimBackCast | the cast rules
      CatchupLeft | a right value: the left reaches a related value or blame

# shared lemmas
EvolveImp | ⊑ and WfWorld transport along W ⟿[ ξs ∣ ξs′ ] W′  uses: AllocImp
CatchupBlame | M ⊑ blame: the left reaches blame
ImprecisionTyping | a ⊑ derivation gives both typings
AllocImp | allocation preserves ⊑ and WfWorld, commutes with Interior
SubstImp | substitution of related values preserves ⊑
InstXImp | inst_X preserves ⊑ (matched and one-sided)
MergeImp | conversion composition preserves conversion ⊑

# type safety (M1), in proof/TypeSafety/; statements in TypeSafety.agda
TypeSafety/Progress | a closed typed term is a value, blame, or steps
TypeSafety/Preservation | steps preserve typing (needs WfCtx)
TypeSafety/Irreducible | values and blame do not step
TypeSafety/Determinism | steps are deterministic up to the fresh rep. var
