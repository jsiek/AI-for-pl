The three refinements of the proposals in design.md (§C9.2, §C9.3):

1. **D30's `cast⊑` cannot use `drop k O`.**  For `∀Y. gen Z. p` (HRm,
   HR) `k = 1`, and `drop 1 [X, Y] = [Y]` opens the cast value's `∀`
   at `Y` instead of `X`.  The premise openings must follow the
   coercion's binder layers: a `∀` layer passes its slot to the cast
   value, a `gen` layer consumes its slot (`CastOpen`, TwoGen's
   `CastClaimG`).  This is also TwoGen (ii) (one consumption per gen
   layer) for free.
2. **The permission of an opened type variable sits at `⊑⟪⟫`**, where
   its opening is created, not at `Λ⊑`.  It pays with the OPENED
   interior index.  Then TwoGen (i) (a `cast⊑cast` that pops) is not
   needed: G0, G2m, HRm and N2.Merged derive without it.
3. **TwoGen (iii) is needed** for G2, HR and N2.TwoCast.  In D30 it is
   a slot of the index: a slot opens the next left `∀` at a right type
   variable, or SKIPS it (left-only, `X⊑★`).  A skip is created only at
   `⊑⟪⟫` and only for a left gen-cast value, and a later `⊑⟪⟫` may
   FILL a carried skip with a new opening.  This is simpler than
   TwoGen's encoding (§2.4).

Two metatheory obligations move rather than vanish (§8): D28's CastFun
κ-weakening (R12) becomes a κ-weakening at `Wrap` into a permitting
boundary.  And a `Merge` of a right hide with an inner rejoin can turn
a permitting join into a continuing type variable.

## 1. The rule set

The variant `_∣_⊢_⊑ᴰ_∶[_]_` has 15 rules, one per HEAD rule.  World:

```
W = (Δ, Δ′, Ω, η, η′, ϱ, κ)            (no π)
W +κ K   =   W with K added to κ        (Agda `_+κ_`)
```

The Agda reuses `ImprecisionWorld.World` and never reads its `πʷ`
field: not in the index, the rules, the side relations or `WfWorldᴰ`.
Every world of every derivation below has `πʷ = []`.  Deleting the
field is mechanical; keeping it lets the variant reuse `Interior`,
`ConversionInterior`, `CtxImp` and `ConvImp` unchanged.

**The index.**  `O` is a list of slots, next first.  A slot is a right
type variable `Y` (open the next left `∀` at `Y`) or `_` (skip):

```
  A ⊑_W^[] A′          =  μ ⊢ η(A) ⊑ η′(A′)
  ∀X.A ⊑_W^(Y·O) A′    =  A[X:=Y] ⊑_W^O A′
  ∀X.A ⊑_W^(_·O) A′    =  A ⊑^O A′ with X left-only (X⊑★),
                           A not a variable, X ∈ A          (as ∀⊑)
```

(Agda `OpenO`, `_⊑ᴰ⟨_∣_⟩_`.)  Congruence (`x⊑x`, `κ⊑κ`, `ƛ⊑ƛ`, `·⊑·`),
`blame⊑`, `cast⊑cast`, `Λ⊑Λ`, `ν⊑ν`, `ν⊑` and `⟪⟫⊑⟪⟫` are HEAD's
rules at `O = []`.

**Casts.**  No cast rule changes the world.

```
  W ∣ γ ⊢ M ⊑ M′ : B ⊑_W^Oₚ A′
  p : B ⇒ A
  p opens O to Oₚ over M
  ──────────────────────────────── (cast⊑)
  W ∣ γ ⊢ M ⟨p⟩ ⊑ M′ : A ⊑_W^O A′

  W ∣ γ ⊢ M ⊑ M′ : A ⊑_W^O B′
  p′ : B′ ⇒ A′
  ──────────────────────────────── (⊑cast)
  W ∣ γ ⊢ M ⊑ M′ ⟨p′⟩ : A ⊑_W^O A′
```

```
  ──────────────────────── (co-plain)
  p opens [] to [] over M

  M a value    p opens O to Oₚ over M
  ──────────────────────────────────── (co-∀)
  ∀X.p opens s·O to s·Oₚ over M

  M a value    p opens O to Oₚ over M
  ──────────────────────────────────── (co-gen)
  gen X.p opens s·O to Oₚ over M
```

**Type abstraction.**  `Λ⊑`'s binder is fresh, a join, or claim-rep
(`Bind`).  Fresh and claim-rep (D29) are HEAD's, at `O = []`.  The join
is D27's pop, consuming the next opening:

```
  W[X:α ↦ Y] ∣ γ ⊢ V ⊑ M′ : A ⊑^O B′
  α fresh    A not a variable    X ∈ A
  Y bound to β:=★, right-only
  ──────────────────────────────────────── (Λ⊑, join)
  W ∣ γ ⊢ ΛX.V ⊑ M′ : ∀X.A ⊑_W^(Y·O) B′
```

**Boundaries.**  `joined(δ ∥ δ′)` is the set of right rep. vars `β`
such that, inside, a type variable bound to `β` is joined to a left type
variable and one of the two is introduced by this boundary (a matched
fresh pair, or a rejoin through `ϱ`; Agda `JoinRep`, `jr-join`).

```
  W[δ ∥ δ′] = Wᵢ
  K ⊆ joined(δ ∥ δ′)
  Aᵢ ⊑_Wᵢ A′ᵢ                                     (the join pays)
  Wᵢ +κ K ∣ [] ⊢ M ⊑ M′ : Aᵢ ⊑ A′ᵢ
  c : Aᵢ ⇒ A    c′ : A′ᵢ ⇒ A′    Wᶜ ⊢ c ⊑ c′
  ─────────────────────────────────────────── (⟪⟫⊑⟪⟫)
  W ∣ γ ⊢ [δ] M ⟨c⟩ ⊑ [δ′] M′ ⟨c′⟩ : A ⊑ A′

  W[δ ∥ ·] = Wᵢ
  K ⊆ joined(δ ∥ ·)
  Aᵢ ⊑_Wᵢ^O A′                                    (the join pays)
  Wᵢ +κ K ∣ [] ⊢ M ⊑ M′ : Aᵢ ⊑^O A′
  c : Aᵢ ⇒ A
  O = [] or (M simple and c has a ∀ per slot of O)
  every −X^α in δ with X ∈ A: no partner of α in ϱ is in κ    (R1′)
  ─────────────────────────────────────── (⟪⟫⊑)
  W ∣ γ ⊢ [δ] M ⟨c⟩ ⊑ M′ : A ⊑_W^O A′

  W[· ∥ δ′] = Wᵢ
  Oᵢ = the slots O seen inside δ′, with the new slots N filled in
  N: openings of type variables δ′ introduces, and skips
     (a skip only for a left gen-cast value);  N = [] or M a value
  each opened Y of Oᵢ: right-only, bound to β:=★, no left partner
     bound to a type variable in scope;  the opened Y are distinct
  K ⊆ joined(· ∥ δ′) ∪ { the rep. var of each new opening }
  A ⊑_Wᵢ^Oᵢ A′ᵢ                                   (the join pays)
  Wᵢ +κ K ∣ [] ⊢ M ⊑ M′ : A ⊑^Oᵢ A′ᵢ
  c′ : A′ᵢ ⇒ A′
  ─────────────────────────────────────── (⊑⟪⟫)
  W ∣ γ ⊢ M ⊑ [δ′] M′ ⟨c′⟩ : A ⊑_W^O A′
```

Every boundary rule also takes `Wᵢ +κ K` well formed (`WfWorldᴰ`:
HEAD's `WfWorld` without `wf-pending`, `wf-distinct`).  `Wᶜ ⊢ c ⊑ c′`
is HEAD's (D17, D18) and reads the EXTERIOR κ, so R2 is unchanged.
"The O seen inside δ′" is `CarriedS`: an opening continues by `toExt`,
a skip continues.  `Fill` keeps the carried slots in order, lets a new
opening replace a carried skip (left to right), and appends the
remaining new slots.

## 2. The design decisions

### 2.1 Where the permission of an opened type variable sits

D28′ says a pushed type variable pays "at the join".  Under D30 the join
of an opening with a left `Λ` happens at `Λ⊑`.  The variant puts the
permission and the payment at `⊑⟪⟫`, where the opening is created:

- The opening is the join at the level of the index.  `⊑⟪⟫` is a term
  binder (a boundary entry), so the world still changes only at term
  binders.
- A left gen value consumes its opening at a cast, which binds no term
  and may not change the world.  With the permission at `Λ⊑`, G0, G2m,
  HRm and N2 would need TwoGen (i).  With it at `⊑⟪⟫` they do not.
- The payment reads the opened left type against the boundary's
  interior type at `X⊑X`.  That is what a payment at `Λ⊑` would read,
  up to the `∀` conversions and `∀` casts between the two.  C4, C4g and
  the hunt (§6) show the payment is not vacuous: their openings fail it.
- `Λ⊑`'s join then needs nothing beyond `Join1` (HEAD's `Open1` without
  the π update).  No corpus derivation wants a permission at `Λ⊑`.

### 2.2 `cast⊑` follows the coercion, not a count

HRm's left value is

```
(ΛX. (λx:X. (λy:★. x)))⟨∀Y. (gen Z. (id(Y) → (Z! → id(Y))))⟩^[]
```

The merged Inst boundary opens `K2 = ∀X.∀Y.X→Y→X` at `[X, Y]`.  The
cast's target has 2 outer `∀`s and its source 1, so D30's display gives
`k = 1` and `drop 1 [X, Y] = [Y]`.  But the source `∀` is the cast's
`∀Y` layer, which belongs to the opening `X`.  With `[Y]` the `Λ` would
face `Y→★→Y` against the right's `X→★→X`.  `CastOpen` passes `X` to the
`Λ` (`co-∀`) and consumes `Y` at the gen layer (`co-gen`), so HRm
derives (`TwoGenᴰ.bodyH`).

### 2.3 TwoGen (i) is not needed

G0's left gen value `(λx:★. 5)⟨gen X. (X! → id(ℕ))⟩` against the right's
Inst boundary `+X^α`, under D28′ + D30 (`TwoGenᴰ.G0ᴰ.g0-final`):

```
⊑cast  ⟨id(★) → id(ℕ)⟩                       ∀X.X→ℕ ⊑ ★→ℕ
  ⊑⟪⟫  +X^α: open at X, permit α;  pays ∀X.X→ℕ ⊑^[X] X→ℕ  (X ⊑ X)
    ⊑cast  ⟨X! → id(ℕ)⟩                       ∀X.X→ℕ ⊑^[X] ★→ℕ  (X⊑★: α permitted)
      cast⊑  gen: consumes X (co-gen)         ★→ℕ ⊑ ★→ℕ
        ⊑⟪⟫  −X^α                             ★→ℕ ⊑ ★→ℕ
          ƛ⊑ƛ, κ⊑κ
```

G2m, HRm and N2.Merged are the same with two openings `[X, Y]` and
`K = [α, β]`.

### 2.4 TwoGen (iii) as a slot

G2's right nests `[+Y^β]` outside `[+X^α]`, with the cast
`⟨id(★) → (id(Y) → id(★))⟩` between them (TwoGen.md §1 renders the
run).  Under the variant (`TwoGenᴰ.twoCast`, `g2-final`):

```
⊑cast  ⟨id(★) → (id(★) → id(★))⟩                    K2 ⊑ ★→★→★
  ⊑⟪⟫  +Y^β: new slots [_, Y], permit β
       pays K2 ⊑^[_,Y] ★→Y→★  (X left-only against ★, Y ⊑ Y)
    ⊑cast  ⟨id(★) → (id(Y) → id(★))⟩               K2 ⊑^[_,Y] ★→Y→★
      ⊑⟪⟫  +X^α: carried [_, Y], X FILLS the skip: [X, Y]; permit α
           pays K2 ⊑^[X,Y] X→Y→X
        G2m's body (§2.3)
```

TwoGen's V2 encoded the skip as a nondeterministic reading of the index
(`OpenImpS`) plus a new-first push (`pv-new`), so every rule whose world
might hold pending type variables took an `OkIx` premise.  Here a skip
is a value of the index, created by `⊑⟪⟫` only (guarded by
`GenCastValue`), consumed by a gen layer (`co-gen` takes any slot) or
filled by `Fill`.  No other rule mentions it.

### 2.5 R1′

R1′ (`UnbindOK′`) reads the boundary's exterior type: `ok-hidden` when
the unbound rep. var's type variable does not occur in it, else HEAD's
`ok-unbind`.  A seal `[−X^α] V ⟨−X⟩` always has exterior `X`, so R1′ is
R1 on seals; it relaxes only pure hides (conversion `Id(A)`, `X ∉ A`),
which create no left `X`-value.  P4h's crossΛ hide needs it
(`P4hᴰ.H⊑`; R1 rejects that step, `P4hᴰ.r1-rejects`).

## 6. The hunt

The new freedoms are: a permission chosen at a join (matched, rejoin,
or a new opening), R1′'s relaxation, openings consumed by casts, and
skips.  Candidates:

- **A gen-valued C4 (mechanized).**  Sources (UNRELATED,
  `PE.C4.source-unrelated`):

  ```
  L   ((λx:★. x : ∀X.X→X) : ★→★) 5 : ℕ
  R   ((ΛX. λx:X. (x : ★)) : ★→★) 5 : ℕ
  ```

  Initial cast terms:

  ```
  L₀  ((λx:★. x)⟨gen X. (X! → X?ℓ0)⟩^[]⟨inst Y. (Y?ℓ0 → Y!)⟩^[] 5⟨ℕ!⟩^[])⟨ℕ?ℓ0⟩^[]
  R₀  ((ΛX. (λx:X. x⟨X!⟩^[X:★∼X∼★]))⟨inst Y. (Y?ℓ0 → id(★))⟩^[] 5⟨ℕ!⟩^[])⟨ℕ?ℓ0⟩^[]
  ```

  The left answers 5 and never blames (`Hunt.Lg₀-answers`,
  `Lg₀-never-blames`); the right blames (`PE.C4.R₂-blames`).  The
  left's `L₀` against the right's state 2

  ```
  (([+X^α] (λx:X. x⟨X!⟩^[X:★∼X∼★]) ⟨−X → id(★)⟩)⟨id(★) → id(★)⟩^[] 5⟨ℕ!⟩^[])⟨ℕ?ℓ0⟩^[]
  ```

  is related in no top-level world (`Hunt.c4gen-unrelated`).  Every
  freedom is available to it: `⊑⟪⟫` may open the gen value's `∀` at X
  and permit α, may skip, and the gen layer may consume.  Each fails at
  the payment: `X→X ⊑ X→★` at X⊑X, `★→★ ⊑ X→★`, or (skip) `X ⊑ X′` with
  X left-only.

- **Permission at a join (argued).**  Under a permission a right `★`
  term may face a left `X` term.  For the right to blame where the left
  does not, a right check `X?` must meet a value tagged `G ≠ X`.  The
  `G`-typed right term that produced it faces a left term of a type
  `⊑ G`: never `X` (`X ⊑ ℕ` fails), so the left also has a `G`-typed
  term there, and it can become `X`-typed again only through its own
  seal.  A seal against a right `★` value is the payload view (R1′:
  its exterior is X, and the partner is permitted) or the ★ clause
  (R2).  This is C5's shape, which stays dead.  A join whose payment
  is vacuous (interior `ℕ`) gives nothing more: the argument above does
  not use the interface.

- **R1′ (argued).**  R1′ admits only boundaries whose exterior type does
  not mention the unbound rep. var.  Their conversion seals nothing (a
  seal of X has exterior X), so no left `X`-value comes out of them.
  The left's own Wrap duals of such a hide are binds (R1 vacuous).

- **Openings consumed by casts, skips (argued).**  A consumed opening
  relates the value under the gen to the right at the gen's source type
  (as HEAD's `cc-gen`); the permission it carries was paid with the
  opened type against the right's interface at X⊑X.  A skip is
  type imprecision's `∀⊑` at one position.

- **Right value against left divergence (argued).**  D28′ and D30 add
  no left-only rule and change no congruence rule; every left
  application still faces a right application.  So the variant relates
  no new pair whose left can diverge while its right converges.

## 8. Lemma impact (STATEMENTS-CORE.md; argued)

- **Pre W, DGG, `RelatedValues`.**  Lose `πʷ W ≡ []`; keep
  `κʷ W ≡ []` and `WfWorld W` (the check fact `hasPP-chk` needs
  `wd-joint`).
- **M1 MorSide, M2 MorImp, M3 EvolveMor.**  The `Opens` / pending parts
  (`PendingMor`) go: openings are right positions in the index and are
  renamed with the right side.  "κ may grow" remains, now only at
  boundaries.
- **M19 SimBackApp, M24 CatchupCast (CastFun).**  No grant moves, so
  `castfun-grant` and `R12 (β ∷ κ)` go.
- **M20 SimBackCast (TagUntag).**  No grant disappears: the drop lemma
  (PermissionsR §4.3) goes.
- **M16 SimApp / M19 SimBackApp (Wrap): a NEW κ-weakening.**  Wrap puts
  the argument `W` into the dual `[−δ] W ⟨c⟩` INSIDE the function's
  boundary, whose interior world carries that boundary's `K`.  `W`'s
  derivation must be re-read at the larger κ, and R1′ and R2 are
  anti-monotone in κ.  What can fail are the parts of `W`'s derivation
  that use R1′ (a payload view) or R2 (a ★ clause) at a rep. var whose
  partner is in `K`.  When the payload view is at the argument's top,
  the right's dual is a seal of the joined type variable and the pair
  can be re-related matched (`⟪⟫⊑⟪⟫`, seal ⊑ seal), as in P4k
  (`P4kᴰ.wrap`).  A general lemma is open.  So D28's R12 moves from
  CastFun to Wrap; it does not disappear.
- **M14 MergeImp, M6 InteriorMerge, M15 RightMergePending: the
  permission of a merged rejoin.**  A right hide `[−X^α]` merged with an
  inner rejoin `[+X^α]` makes X continuing, and `JoinRep` admits only
  type variables a boundary introduces.  A permission the inner rejoin
  chose (and paid for) is then not available to the merged boundary.
  In every corpus Merge (P4c R7–R10, R2c) the permission comes from an
  outer join, so nothing is lost.  Two ways to close it: let `JoinRep`
  also admit a type variable that an entry of the boundary binds and
  that is joined inside (the merged `[−X, +X]` binds X), paying the
  same index; or show the permission can move out to the boundary that
  first joined X.  Not checked.
- **PushInstR, RightMergePending, PushCompose, WfPop, PopInstX,
  CatchupRightπ (D27's new MAJOR).**  Their π bookkeeping becomes slot
  bookkeeping in the index (`CarriedS`, `Fill`); `WfPop` becomes the
  `SlotOK` premise of `⊑⟪⟫`.  The payment of a new opening is the
  opened interior index at κ without the opening's rep. var, which is
  exactly the index HEAD's push already needed (a pending type variable
  was `X⊑X` unless permitted, D28).  So adding `K = [β]` costs no new
  index; the derivation inside must then hold at the larger κ (the
  κ-weakening of the Wrap item).  With `K = []` it is HEAD's case.
- **M13 InstXImpL, M12 InstXImp2.**  A left TyBeta catching up with a
  joined opening is HEAD's (`allocᴸ⇔` for `Join1`).  A matched TyBeta
  (`ν⊑ν` to `⟪⟫⊑⟪⟫`) may now choose `K = [αᴿ]`; its payment is the
  interior index HEAD's contractum already needed (αᴿ is new, hence
  unpermitted), so it costs no new index, and the inside needs the
  κ-weakening above.  P4k and P4h are the cases where the choice is
  needed.
- **M22 SimBackBlame, M26 CastRedexNoBlame.**  C1–C5 and C4g stay
  dead (§5), and the hunt adds a gen-valued C4.  No counterexample is
  known.
- **New skeleton holes.**  The `K`/payment premises of the three
  boundary rules, and `co-gen` with a continuation (the `cast⊑` case of
  Sim/SimBack when the left's gen layer consumes an opening).
