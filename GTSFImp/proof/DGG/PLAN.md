  FALSE: `CenterCrossingProbe.agda` derives its premise at the real

  a moved target pivot on a right-side chain forces the sealed source

- The reachability investigation (ReachabilityScreen/Catalog, 28

  identity-embedding worlds (`compile-preserves-imprecision²`), all
  screened pairs stay clean, the only hand-built suspect is outside the
  compile image, and every deep-dive checkpoint needed only parked
  world evolution.
- Decision (2026-08-08): repair candidate (c) — restrict to
  parked-reachable worlds. The rebasing design inside
  `CastTermImprecision2` (a recent addition for Example 12) MAY be
  adjusted, provided compile monotonicity still holds and all examples

## The parked discipline (target of M1/M2)

- `ParkedWorld`: inductive closure of `initialWorld` under parked
  extensions — both-bind (paired allocation, mark `X⊑X`, shared fresh
  center), right-only bind (fresh center), and LEFT-only bind (fresh
  center, mark `X⊑★`; needed for the one-sided source rules `Λ⊑ᴳ`/
  `[]⊑ᴳ`, e.g. `(ΛX.λx:X.x)[ℕ] 5 ⊑ (λx:★.x) 5` — the precise side
  allocates, the imprecise side does not). Reachability IS the
  datatype; geometry (no crossing, old target centers stable) becomes
  lemmas by induction.
- `ParkedEvolve χᴸ χᴿ W W′`: two-sided evolution witness tying store
  changes to world extension; generalizes stage-1 `WorldExtendᴿ`.
  One `pe-both` covers temporally-skewed pairs within a single
  simulation step (left allocates in its step; the right's matching
  `inst` fires during catch-up).
- Asymmetric rebase rule (M2): source pivots may re-park (Example 12,
  decay); old TARGET variables' centers are frozen; fresh target pivots
  enter at `Fin.zero`. Left-only binds do not threaten the asymmetry:
  they mint fresh centers and move nothing old.

## File organization

New work follows the GTSF/proof `Def`/`Proof`/`Lemma` split to enable
separate compilation and parallel proof tracks:

- `...Def.agda` — statements as `Set`-level definitions (no proofs).
- `...Proof.agda` — higher-order workers; import ONLY `Def` files of
  the lemmas they consume (never other `Proof` files).
- `...Lemma.agda` — stitch `Def` + `Proof` into the real inhabitants.

New modules live under `proof/DGG/Parked/`.
