## The well-formedness condition: named uniqueness

```agda
NamedUniqueᴸ W = ∀ {α α′ β}
  → names Δ ∋ᵅ α → names Δ ∋ᵅ α′ → names Δ′ ∋ᵅ β
  → Paired W α β → Paired W α′ β → α ≡ α′
NamedUniqueᴿ W = ∀ {α β β′}
  → names Δ ∋ᵅ α → names Δ′ ∋ᵅ β → names Δ′ ∋ᵅ β′
  → Paired W α β → Paired W α β′ → β ≡ β′
```

Among the rep. vars named on one side, at most one is paired with a
given rep. var named on the other side.  Rep. vars with no name in scope
are unconstrained: store rep. vars, and rep. vars hidden by an unbind.

**Why `Interior` needs it.**  `join-fresh` is a biconditional.  A name
that a boundary introduces must join every name on the other side whose
rep. var is paired with its own:

```
Fresh Θ X ⊎ Fresh Θ′ X′  →  (Joins Wᵢ X X′ ⇔ Paired W α β)
```

An embedding joins a name to at most one name on the other side.  So if
a fresh right `+X′^β` faced two left names whose rep. vars are both
paired with β, no interior world would exist.  The same holds in the
mirror case, for a fresh left name.  Fresh names arise on both sides, so
both directions are needed.  Informally, given `Joint W`, named uniqueness of the
interior contexts (with ϱᵢ = ϱ) is enough for the joins to form a
matching.

**Why it is the weakest condition of this kind.**  It constrains only
pairs that are both named in scope, which are exactly the pairs
`join-fresh` reads.  It admits:

- C12 (`W₁₂ˣ-wf` etc.): the left αᴸ has two right partners, but only one
  of them is named at a time.
- L3d (below): the right αᴿ has two left partners, both of which are
  store rep. vars with no name.

Allocations never name a rep. var, so every evolution step preserves
named uniqueness.  The condition is stated over rep. vars rather than
positions.  Coherence (`WfCtx.name-fn`) makes names injective on rep.
vars, so the two forms agree on well-formed contexts, and the rep. var
form is the weaker one.  It does not cover the order-preservation of the
joins, an existence question that predates D25.

## R3 verdict: `⊕⁺ˢ` is not needed; the plain `⊕⁺` is right

**The general lemma** (`proof/ImprecisionWorld.agda`):

```agda
wf-⊕⁺ : WfWorld W → Δ′ ∋rep β := ★ → NoNamedPartner W β
  → WfWorld (W ⊕⁺ m ^ β)
NoNamedPartner W β = ∀ {α} → names Δ ∋ᵅ α → ¬ Paired W α β
```

The proof takes each field in turn:

- **Joint.** The head is `both (inj₂ here⇔)`, and every pair of W
  survives shifted (`paired-⊕⁺`).
- **Agree.** The pair (0, β) is `abst-★`.  A shifted pair keeps its
  agreement by `⊑ᴿ-ren suc id` (`agree-⊕⁺`).
- **Named.** In the premise world, the only names that are new or
  changed are the left 0 and the right β.  The rep. var 0 is paired only
  with β.  The other left partners of β are excluded by
  `NoNamedPartner`, or are unnamed.

`NoNamedPartner` replaces D13's global `NoLeftPartner` with its scoped
form.  It is not among `∀⊑⟪+⟫`'s premises, and nothing derives it.  If
the DGG needs `WfWorld (W ⊕⁺ m ^ β)` (SimBackProof's hole), the rule
should take it as a premise, either as `NoNamedPartner W β` or directly
as `WfWorld (W ⊕⁺ m ^ β)`, as `⟪⟫⊑⟪⟫` takes `WfWorld Wᵢ`.  **That is a
decision for Jeremy.  TermImprecision is unchanged.**

**L3c/R3c** (`ForallBoundaryFixes.agda` §6).  After the left's TyBeta of
copy 1, the world is W₁ = {(αᴸ, αᴿ)}.  Copy 2's premise world is

```
W₁ ⊕⁺ X⊑X ^ 0 = world (X⊑X ∷ []) (keep []↪) (keep []↪) {(1,0)} {(0,0)}
```

In it, αᴿ has the two left partners 1 (= αᴸ, global) and 0 (lexical)
(`two-partners`).  Only 0 has a left name (`only-0-named`), so a rejoin
of αᴿ inside the premise joins 0 and nothing else.  The premise world is
well formed (`post-premise-wf`).  Under D13 it was not (the old
`old-premise-¬wf`).  `l3c-pre`, `l3c-evolve` (`ev-L⇔`, now without
`no-partner`) and `l3c-post` all check.

**`⊕⁺ˢ` is withdrawn, because it is unsound for D23.**  Payloads mention
rep. vars, and a payload pair may be related through a pair of β.  In
`Wd`, rep. var 1 is ℕ on the left and ★ (= β) on the right, and the two
are paired.  Rep. var 0 holds ` 1 on both sides, and (0, 0) agrees by

```
` 1 ⊑ᴿ ` 1      (α⊑β, through (1, 1))
```

`Wd-wf` and `Wd⁺-wf : WfWorld (Wd ⊕⁺ X⊑X ^ 1)` hold.  `⊕⁺ˢ` drops
(2, 1), and the shifted (1, 0) then has payloads ` 2 / ` 1 with nothing
to relate them: `⊕⁺ˢ-breaks-agreement : ¬ WfWorld (Wd ⊕⁺ˢ X⊑X ^ 1)`.
The old `wf-⊕⁺ˢ` was proved against D23's predecessor, which read
payloads through names.  It does not carry over.

**The "V mentions β's left partner" case.**  This is unchanged in
substance.  Inside the premise, a left `[+X^αᴸ]`, with αᴸ paired with β
and β named on the right, would make two left names partners of β.  The
interior world then fails named uniqueness, and no derivation exists.
ForallBoundaryFixes.md's informal non-reachability argument still
applies.

**R2.**  This is unaffected.  §8 and §9 now read `⊕⁺`:

- `allocᴿ-⊕⁺` is proved, and is simpler than `allocᴿ-⊕⁺ˢ` was.
- `B-admissible` gains a `NoNamedPartner` hypothesis.
- `SimBackInstX` is stated at `⊕⁺`.

  `Pw = W4 ⊕⁺ X⊑X ^ 0` is unchanged.
